== hook | ground: dark
Almost everything / *shrinks*
when it freezes.
Water does / the *opposite,*
and that's why / ice *floats.*
== liquid | ground: dark
In liquid water, / the *molecules*
tumble past each other,
packed close *together.*
== rings | ground: dark
As water freezes,
they lock into *rings,*
and each ring holds
an *empty* space / in the middle.
== room | ground: dark
Same molecules, / more *room.*
Ice ends up about / a tenth *bigger,*
so it's *lighter*
than the water / it came from.
== lake | ground: dark
That's why a *lake*
freezes from the top down,
and the *fish* / survive underneath.
