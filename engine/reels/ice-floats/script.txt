Almost everything shrinks when it freezes. Water does the opposite, and that's why ice floats.

In liquid water, the molecules tumble past each other, packed close together.

As water freezes, they lock into rings, and each ring holds an empty space in the middle.

Same molecules, more room. Ice ends up about a tenth bigger, so it's lighter than the water it came from.

That's why a lake freezes from the top down, and the fish survive underneath.
